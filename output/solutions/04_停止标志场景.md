## 3. 机制研究

**双机制**：
1. **StopSign TrafficRule**（`traffic_rules/stop_sign/`）：检测参考线 stop_sign_overlaps → `BuildStopDecision: stop_s = stop_line_s - config_.stop_distance()` → 停止线前 stop_distance 米
2. **StopSignUnprotectedScenario**（`scenarios/stop_sign_unprotected/`）：PRE_STOP → STOP → CREEP → INTERSECTION_CRUISE 四阶段，含停止计时、蠕行确认安全

**核心参数**：`stop_sign/conf/default_conf.pb.txt` → `stop_distance`
